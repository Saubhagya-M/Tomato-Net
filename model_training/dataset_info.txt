Tomato Leaf Disease Dataset Information

Dataset Source:
PlantVillage Dataset

Total Classes: 10

Classes:
1. Bacterial_spot
2. Early_blight
3. Late_blight
4. Leaf_Mold
5. Septoria_leaf_spot
6. Spider_mites (Two-spotted spider mite)
7. Target_Spot
8. Tomato_Yellow_Leaf_Curl_Virus
9. Tomato_mosaic_virus
10. Healthy

Dataset Description:
This dataset contains images of tomato leaves showing different diseases
and healthy leaves. The images are used to train a deep learning model
to classify tomato plant diseases automatically.

Image Size Used For Training:
224 x 224

Color Format:
RGB

Model Used:
MobileNetV2 (Transfer Learning)

Training Split:
Training: 80%
Validation: 20%

Goal:
To build an AI system that can detect tomato leaf diseases from images
to assist farmers in early diagnosis.
